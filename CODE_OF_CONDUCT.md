# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Contributors and maintainers are expected to participate in a respectful,
welcoming, and constructive way.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and
other contributions that do not align with this code of conduct.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to Reuben Miller.
